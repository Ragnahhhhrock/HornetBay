The tactical map (N) was always the whole bay or nothing, drawn at eighty-eight percent over the world — the sea shimmered through the chart like a double exposure. Both fixed:

  1. Drag to pan. Grab the chart with the mouse and pull it where you want it; the window slides over the whole 115-by-104-kilometre sheet. The gun politely stays silent while you’re dragging the map — no accidental bursts mid-brief.

Ever wondered what the sky looks like from a cable’s length behind a 747? 2026-07-24BUGFIXCONTROLS

Q toggles the menu now: quit out, Q back in a083612

A pilot reported the trap: mid-mission you hit Q to call up the main menu — and there was no way back. The menu slammed down over the cockpit and the only way out was to abandon the sortie and relaunch. The sortie itself was never the problem — your jet waits exactly where you left it while the attract demo borrows the camera — the menu just never offered the way back.

Now it does, two ways. Quit to the menu with Q and you’ll find a new top entry: Q — RESUME FLIGHT, tagged BACK IN THE COCKPIT. Hit Q again (or click it) and the menu folds away mid-frame, mission intact, hands back on the stick. The flag clears the moment you launch a fresh sortie, so there’s no ghost resume waiting after you’ve moved on. The subtle part of the fix: the resume key is polled in the frame loop rather than fired from the key event itself — otherwise the same keypress gets seen by both states and bounces you straight back out to the menu. Fifty-one checks green, and the Bay forgives an accidental Q.

2026-07-23CONTROLSUX

First solo: a quick-start card, a DEMO that demos, and a trackpad stick 9a4a5df

First feedback from a new pilot on a MacBook: "can't get off the ground." Fair — the sim never told you how. Three fixes. Every launch now opens a quick-start card with the handful of keys that get you airborne (W to firewall the throttles, B brakes off, pull back at 150 knots, G gear up, ? for the full manual) — it fades by itself, closes on a click, and has a don't-show-again tick for veterans. The DEMO menu item actually demos now: it hides the menu and plays the attract loop full-screen until any key brings you back (previously it did exactly nothing, since the demo jet was already flying behind the menu). And the mouse stick grows up: press Y, then click the screen to capture the cursor — pointer lock turns relative movement into stick deflection, which is how the Amiga original flew and, crucially, the only way a trackpad can.

2026-07-22BUG FIXCONTROLS

The gun answers SPACE and ENTER — with tracers you can see d0ee2a7

Three fixes in one pass. The gun simply ignored SPACE — only the mouse trigger worked, because the fire code never looked at the keyboard for the Vulcan. Now SPACE and ENTER both fire the selected weapon (hold either for a burst), so weapon select moves to TAB, with 1 / 2 / 3 jumping straight to AMRAAM, Sidewinder or gun. And the tracers themselves are rebuilt: the old bolts were pencil-thin and effectively invisible from the cockpit, so each round now flies as a fat glowing bolt that reads day or night.
