Every menu item is now a link PENDING

“Meet me at the mission select” used to mean a string of instructions — open the game, wait for the menu, press 6. Now it’s a link you paste. Every row of the menu carries its own URL stub, and the address bar tracks you as you move, so wherever you are, what’s in the bar is a link back to exactly here:

  1. /#freeflight — straight to the free-flight map, pick your start point.
  2. /#missions — the SELECTABLE MISSIONS board, and each sortie has its own: /#m1 through /#m7 drop you at that briefing.
  3. /#log — your flight log statistics; /#gallery — the showroom floor; /#manual — the flight manual; /#menu — home.
  4. The toggles answer to links too: /#day and /#night set the hour, /#clear · /#clouds · /#rain · /#storm pick the weather — handy for screenshot hunters.
  5. And the outbound rows: /#blog, /#store, /#analytics, /#print all redirect to their sites, so every menu item — even the ones that leave the sim — has a stub.
  6. Polite in the air: a link clicked mid-sortie never yanks the controls — routing waits for the menu. And if you suspended a sortie with Q, /#resume puts you back in the cockpit.
