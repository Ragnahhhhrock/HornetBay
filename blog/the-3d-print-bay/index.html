The 3D Print Bay: the fleet, on your desk 38702a9

The models in the sim were too good to keep on the far side of the screen. The 3D Print Bay is open:

  1. All 22 models as printable STL files — ten aircraft (Hornet, Falcon, MiG-29, the five SFO heavies, Air Force One, the cruise missile) and twelve vessels (the Enterprise, her three escorts, the supertanker, the cruise liner, the freighter, the sub, and the small traffic). Every one exported straight from the same code that flies and sails it in the sim — low-poly by design, gloriously so.
  2. Print-ready out of the zip. Wheels up, weapons off, scaled in millimetres with the longest dimension sized for a standard print bed, and a PRINTING.txt in every zip with the scale, printed size and slicer settings. Ships print hull-down, no supports needed on most beds.
  3. Priced per model — $2 for the little rescue raft, $4 for the fighters, up to $8 for the Enterprise — or take THE COMPLETE FLEET: all 22 files in one zip for $59, less than half the individual prices.

  5. Checkout and delivery run through Fourthwall (the squadron’s merchant of record, same as the store): the download link arrives by email the moment payment clears. The paid files themselves are never exposed on the site — only the freebie.
  6. Gallery coverage grew to make this possible: the Boeing 707, cruise missile, Shadow Sub and rescue raft all joined the showroom, and every card portrait on the page was captured in the gallery — squadron policy now.
