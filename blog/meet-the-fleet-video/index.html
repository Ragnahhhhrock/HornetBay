Meet the Fleet, in motion: 360° orbits and the Hornet Bay anthem 040292e

The fleet profiles deserved more than stills. The new MEET THE FLEET video puts the camera in a slow 360° orbit around every airliner in the Bay — all sides of the ALLIED 747-400, the LIBERTY AIR 737-400, the PACIFIC EMPRESS DC-10 and the CASCADE AIR MD-90, wheels up over open water. Each orbit opens with a hold on the hero angle while the airline’s name and aircraft type fade in, then swings all the way around the airframe.

The whole thing flies to the Hornet Bay anthem, opening with a genuine in-game flyby whoosh. It ships in two cuts: a 47-second widescreen cut for the Hornet Bay YouTube channel, and a vertical Shorts cut with the specs riding under every aircraft. The four fleet profiles — ALLIED, LIBERTY, PACIFIC EMPRESS, CASCADE — have the full specifications.
