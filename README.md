Launch from **USS Enterprise (CVN-65)** in a living San Francisco Bay: a full carrier air wing cycling the deck around you, escorts, airliners on approach, helicopters on plane guard, P-3s and Seahawks hunting a shadow submarine, and a campaign of intercept missions from a dead-simple visual-ID qual to a nuclear-tipped sprint for the city.

### FLY
- **13-mission campaign** — visual ID, carriers under attack, defectors, SAR, cruise-missile hunts, a spy balloon at Angels 34, a presidential trap under the MISSION ACCOMPLISHED banner, and a blood debt to settle for your wingman. Each mission flown unlocks the next.
- **Free flight** over the bay, day / dusk / night, weather on demand.
- **Carrier qualification** — three clean traps to earn your wings.
- Skilled opponents that actually dogfight: breaks, jinks, split-S, loops and barrel rolls, flares and chaff — they do not fly straight, and they are hard to hit.
- Live map, spectate any aircraft in the area, 2–32× time skip, full flight model with stalls, spins and deep-water swims.

### THE FLEET
Flyable F/A-18, F-14, F-16 and Harrier — plus a full supporting cast: A-6, S-3, E-2C, C-2, P-3, SH-60, MiG-29, Su-27, airliners, warships and one very famous balloon. Every model in the sim is also a free STL on the **[print page](https://hornetbay.com/print)**.

### TECH
Vanilla JavaScript + Three.js, no build step, no frameworks — one `index.html` and a `js/` folder, served as a static site. Low-poly models built in code, Web Audio engine sounds, localStorage saves.

### RUN LOCALLY
Any static file server works:
```sh
npx serve .
# or
python3 -m http.server
```
Then open the page and press **ENTER**.
